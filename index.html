The Maison Story

The Spirit of BHŪMI

"True luxury is not about gatekeeping elegance. It is about honoring the craft, cherishing the materials, and welcoming every soul who values beauty."

Founded on the principle of Bhūmi (the Sanskrit word for Earth and fundamental grounding), our atelier creates an effortless bridge between modern, accessible essentials and rare haute horology and fine jewellery.

Each garment uses non-toxic, ethically harvested linens and mulberry silks. Our jewellery uses 100% recycled precious metals and conflict-free lab and natural diamonds. Our watches are calibrated to exacting chronometric standards.
